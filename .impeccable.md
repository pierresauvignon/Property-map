## Design Context

### Users
Two people (Alex and Sam) house-hunting together. Used on a laptop
at home to compare and score, and on a phone in the car or on a doorstep to
check "which one is this, what did we think of it". Mobile use is glanceable,
one-handed, often in daylight.

### Brand Personality
Private, calm, editorial. A shared notebook, not a listings portal.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light paper surfaces, muted map tiles, one warm accent for price/actions,
per-person colours (Alex blue, Sam green). French copy throughout. Not a
Leboncoin/SeLoger clone; no marketing chrome.

### Design Principles
- The map is the product; controls never cover what they describe.
- One-handed on phone: 44px targets, thumb-reach bottom sheet.
- Nothing is amputated on mobile; it is re-laid-out.
- Every word earns its place (French, terse).
